## Cup spec used

- 16 oz single-wall paper cup: top Ø 90 mm, bottom Ø 60 mm, height 136 mm
- Fan: outer radius 410.47 mm, inner radius 273.65 mm, angle 39.47° (+ 7 mm glue overlap), slant height 136.82 mm
- Safe zone: 4 mm under the rim, 8 mm above the base fold. All important art sits inside it.
- Colours: forest #143F31 (≈ C85 M45 Y75 K45), gold #D9B869 (≈ C15 M28 Y70 K0, or foil), cream petals #F3EBDD

## Before sending to print

1. **Ask your cup supplier for their 16 oz die-line** and compare. Cup moulds differ; if their sizes differ, change `CUP` at the top of `scripts/cup-print.ts` and run `pnpm cup:print` again.
2. **Replace the typed "CHACHA" with the final leaf-A logo** once the vector exists. The wordmark here is set in Plus Jakarta Sans as a placeholder.
3. Ask the printer whether they want the colours converted to CMYK themselves (they usually do) and whether gold will be CMYK or foil.
